FAQ

Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

That message appears for new apps that are not code-signed yet. Click More info and then Run anyway. LiveShive runs fully on your computer and never uploads your streams.

Will it work on my old laptop?

Yes. On PCs with less than 8 GB RAM or 4 threads LiveShive switches to Light mode: smaller speech model, 720p clips and the fastest encoder. Clips take a little longer, but it keeps up.

Which streams are supported?

YouTube, Twitch, Kick, Facebook, Rumble and 1000+ other sites, plus raw .m3u8, RTMP and local video files. Members-only streams work with browser cookies enabled in Settings.

Does it support Hindi, Urdu and Hinglish?

Yes. Auto mode keeps English captions in English and writes Hindi or Urdu speech in Roman Hinglish. You can also choose Hindi or Urdu script.

When is the Mac version coming?

The Mac version is in the works. It will appear on this page and on the releases page as soon as it is ready.

Is it free?

Yes, LiveShive is free to download and use.
